# GenPet development

- Before design, prompt or runtime work, read [docs/DESIGN.md](docs/DESIGN.md): the design idea, the principles and the framework. Make decisions from it, and keep the change consistent with its principles. The product principles the plugin runs on are in [plugins/genpet/skills/genpet/principles.md](plugins/genpet/skills/genpet/principles.md).
- Follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for where to edit, records and hosts, and testing with a real Agent.
- When a change alters the design, update docs/DESIGN.md and docs/DESIGN.zh-CN.md together.
- Before a version bump, release check, publish or install verification, read and follow [CONTRIBUTING.md, "Releasing"](CONTRIBUTING.md#releasing).
- Do not push, tag or publish without the maintainer's explicit approval of that change. Approval of earlier changes does not cover later ones.
