# genpet-story

Use [the main skill](../genpet/SKILL.md), StoryDesigner and [story flow](../genpet/flows/stories.md). Bind the selected Pet by ID, resume pending work before a new manual trigger, read actual context, then decide the story directly and keep saved results on resume. The story decides the everyday look (full set), transformation, item/home, memory or growth changes; complete accepted visuals and same-cloud readback. Present only actual content and final media. No Pet means direct guidance through /genpet, not implicit adoption; this explicit request does not require a repeated generic confirmation.
